# Home Screen - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
The **Home Screen** (Dashboard) is the main control center of **Piggy Ledger**. It provides an instant, unified overview of your entire financial life at a glance. Designed to be clean, intuitive, and highly encouraging, the Home Screen allows you to keep track of your money without feeling overwhelmed by complex numbers.

---

## Core Components on the Home Screen

### 1. Header & Greeting
- **Visual Design**: Personal greeting tailored to the time of day, displaying your name and active streak status.
- **Purpose**: Welcomes you back to the app and establishes habit consistency through daily engagement.
- **Interactions**:
  - Tap the **Profile / Settings** icon in the top corner to jump directly into App Settings, Security, Language preferences, or Backup options.
  - Tap the **Streak Badge** to view your full habit streak board, freeze badges, and unlocked achievements.

---

### 2. Net Worth & Summary Cards
- **Visual Design**: High-contrast, easy-to-read cards displaying **Total Net Worth**, **Total Balance**, and monthly cash flow (**Income vs. Expenses**).
- **Purpose**: Gives an accurate real-time picture of your net worth across all cash wallets, bank accounts, and digital wallets combined.
- **Key Metrics**:
  - **Net Worth**: Total Assets minus unpaid debts or loans.
  - **Total Balance**: Real monetary balance sitting in active liquid accounts.
  - **Monthly Income & Expense**: Total money in vs. total money out for the active calendar month.
- **Pro Tip**: Hidden balance toggle — tap the eye icon to obscure or show monetary values when viewing your screen in public places.

---

### 3. Quick Action Bar
- **Visual Design**: Prominent, single-tap floating and action buttons.
- **Primary Actions**:
  - **+ Add Transaction**: Instant logging for income or expenses with account selection, category tags, and notes.
  - **Transfer**: Move funds directly between two existing accounts (e.g., from Bank Account to Vodafone Cash).
  - **Quick Scan / Pending SMS**: Instantly jump to pending SMS-parsed transactions needing approval.

---

### 4. Streak & Gamification Banner
- **Visual Design**: Animated mascot banner showing your continuous daily tracking streak.
- **Benefit**: Financial mindfulness relies on regular tracking. The streak feature rewards you for logging transactions daily or simply opening the app to check your balances.

---

### 5. Recent Activity Feed
- **Visual Design**: Chronological list of recent income, expense, and transfer records with distinct category icons and color-coded amounts (Green for Income, Red for Expense, Neutral for Transfer).
- **Interactions**:
  - Tap any transaction to view full details, edit date/amount/category, or delete it.
  - Tap **See All** to open the complete transaction ledger with search and filter controls.

---

## Why Use the Home Screen Daily?
1. **Financial Awareness**: Prevents unexpected end-of-month surprises by showing accurate real-time balances.
2. **Fast Logging**: Access the transaction logger in one tap without navigating complex sub-menus.
3. **Habit Building**: Visual streak rewards make personal finance feel rewarding instead of tedious.
