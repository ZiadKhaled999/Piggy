# Creating & Managing Accounts - Step-by-Step Guide

## Overview
Piggy Ledger lets you set up customized accounts matching your real-life financial setup. You can add popular bank accounts with built-in logos or create custom wallets.

---

## How to Add a New Account

### Step-by-Step Process:
1. Navigate to the **Accounts** screen from the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the **+ Add Account** button.
3. **Select Account Type**:
   - **Cash Wallet**: For physical money in your pocket or safe.
   - **Bank Account**: CIB, NBE, Banque Misr, AlexBank, HSBC, Arab Bank, QNB, ADIB, etc.
   - **Mobile Wallet**: Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash, WE Pay.
   - **Payment App**: InstaPay, PayPal.
   - **Custom Account**: Any custom store credit, savings account, or vault.
4. **Enter Account Name**: Give the account a clear title (e.g., `CIB Savings`, `Personal Cash`, `Vodafone Cash Main`).
5. **Initial Starting Balance**: Enter the current real balance in this account today.
6. **Select Brand Logo / Color**: Choose from official pre-built logos (CIB, NBE, Vodafone Cash, etc.) or select a custom icon and theme color.
7. **Default Account Toggle**: Set this as your default primary account for fast transaction logging.
8. Tap **Save Account**.

---

## Editing & Customizing Existing Accounts
- Tap the **Gear / Edit** icon on any account card to:
  - Update account name, logo, or color theme.
  - Adjust opening balance.
  - Set as default account.
  - **Archive Account**: Hide accounts you no longer actively use (e.g., closed bank accounts) while retaining past transaction history for analytics.

---

## Deleting an Account
- If you delete an account, Piggy Ledger will prompt you to choose whether to permanently erase all associated transactions or reassign past records to another account.
