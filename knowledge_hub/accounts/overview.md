# Accounts Screen - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
The **Accounts Screen** in **Piggy Ledger** is your financial command center for managing every place your money lives. In modern financial life, money is rarely stored in a single spot—you might have cash in hand, bank accounts (e.g., CIB, NBE, Banque Misr, HSBC), digital mobile wallets (e.g., Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash, WE Pay), or payment services (InstaPay).

Piggy Ledger lets you organize, track, and manage all of these accounts under one unified roof.

---

## Core Capabilities of the Accounts Screen

### 1. Account Portfolio Overview
- View total liquid balance combined across all accounts.
- Filter by account type: **Cash**, **Bank Accounts**, **Mobile Wallets**, or **Custom Vaults**.

---

### 2. Multi-Account Management
- Add, edit, archive, or delete accounts.
- Customize account icons, bank logos, custom brand colors, and initial balances.

---

### 3. Transaction History per Account
- Tap any specific account to inspect its individual ledger history, isolated from other accounts.
- Filter income, expenses, and inter-account transfers for that account specifically.

---

### 4. Account Identifiers & SMS Mapping
- Connect SMS sender names (e.g., `CIB`, `V-Cash`, `NBE`) to automatically attribute parsed incoming messages to the correct account.

---

## Why Organize Accounts in Piggy Ledger?
- **Zero Confusion**: Know exactly how much money is available in your digital wallet versus your main bank checking account before making a payment.
- **Accurate Auditing**: Easily compare your actual bank app balances with your Piggy Ledger records to spot forgotten subscriptions or unrecorded expenses.
