# Screen Protection & Privacy Mode - User Guide

## Overview
Screen protection prevents unauthorized visual access to your financial records, whether through Android background snapshot previews or shoulder-surfing in public spaces.

---

## 1. Screen Protection (Prevent Screenshots & Task Swapper Leak)

### How to Enable:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Security & Privacy**.
2. Toggle on **Screen Protection / Block Screenshots**.

### What It Does:
- **Disables Screenshots**: Prevents screen capture apps, malware, or accidental keypresses from taking screenshots of your accounts or balances.
- **Task Switcher Privacy Blur**: When switching between apps, Android's multi-tasking view displays a blurred shield instead of a clear screenshot of your financial dashboard.

---

## 2. Hide Balances (Privacy Masking)

### How to Enable:
- Tap the **Eye Icon** on the Home screen or enable **Hide Balances by Default** in Security Settings.

### What It Does:
- Replaces monetary amounts across Net Worth cards, Account Balances, and Transaction lists with masked bullets (`EGP ••••••`).
- Tap the eye icon anytime to momentarily reveal values when safe.

---

## When Should You Enable These Features?
- **Public Transport / Public Workspaces**: Prevents onlookers from seeing your net worth or monthly earnings.
- **Shared Devices**: Protects your financial privacy if family members or children occasionally use your smartphone.
