# Piggy Ledger - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
Welcome to **Piggy Ledger**! This folder serves as the root index for the Piggy Ledger application ecosystem. Here, you will find high-level concepts regarding what Piggy Ledger is, its core mission, how it empowers everyday users, and best practices for mastering your personal finances.

---

## What is Piggy Ledger?
**Piggy Ledger** is a privacy-first, offline-ready personal finance app designed to help individuals, freelancers, families, and small business owners manage cash flow, bank accounts, digital mobile wallets, debt payoffs, and savings goals effortlessly.

---

## Core Pillars of Piggy Ledger

1. **Privacy & Offline First**: Your money data belongs to you. Records are stored locally on your device with biometric protection.
2. **Effortless Automation**: Smart bank SMS detection eliminates tedious manual entry.
3. **Holistic Wealth Tracking**: Manages checking accounts, cash, digital wallets, debts, and savings goals in one unified app.
4. **Gamified Motivation**: Habit streaks, mascot moods, and visual progress bars turn budgeting into an enjoyable daily routine.
5. **Universal Accessibility**: Native Arabic and English support with RTL layout design.
