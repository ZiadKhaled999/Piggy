# Goal Deposits & Withdrawals - User Guide

## Managing Goal Funds
Once a goal is created, you will deposit money into it regularly as your savings grow, or withdraw funds if you need access to them.

---

## How to Deposit Money into a Goal

### Step-by-Step Deposit Process:
1. Tap on the desired **Goal Card** from the My Goals screen.
2. Tap the green **Deposit / Add Money** button.
3. Enter the **Deposit Amount**.
4. Select the **Funding Source Account** (e.g., Bank Account, InstaPay, Cash).
5. Add an optional **Note** (e.g., "Monthly bonus deposit").
6. Tap **Confirm Deposit**.

### What Happens Behind the Scenes:
- The goal's progress bar increases immediately.
- The selected funding account balance is updated to reflect the transferred amount.
- The total progress percentage updates in real time on your dashboard.

---

## How to Withdraw Money from a Goal

### Step-by-Step Withdrawal Process:
1. Tap on the **Goal Card** you wish to withdraw from.
2. Tap the **Withdraw Funds** option.
3. Enter the amount you are withdrawing.
4. Select the **Destination Account** where the money should be returned (e.g., Cash or Checking Account).
5. Specify the reason (e.g., "Partial purchase" or "Emergency spending").
6. Tap **Confirm Withdrawal**.

---

## Completing a Goal 🎉
When your saved balance reaches 100% of the target amount:
- Piggy Ledger displays a celebratory victory mascot screen!
- You can mark the goal as **Completed & Archived** or transfer the full saved amount directly to a designated spending account for your purchase.
