# Creating a Goal - Step-by-Step Guide

## Overview
Creating a savings goal in Piggy Ledger takes less than 30 seconds. This guide details every field and configuration option available when building a goal.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open the Goal Creator
- Navigate to the **Goals** tab from the bottom navigation bar.
- Tap the **+ Create Goal** button or the floating action button.

---

### Step 2: Fill in Goal Details
1. **Goal Title**: Give your goal a clear, inspiring name (e.g., `Emergency Fund`, `New Laptop`, `Summer Vacation`, `Car Down Payment`).
2. **Target Amount**: Enter the total monetary amount needed to fulfill the goal (e.g., `$1,500` or `EGP 50,000`).
3. **Target Completion Date**: Select your target deadline using the calendar picker. Piggy Ledger will calculate how much you need to save daily or monthly to reach it on time!
4. **Initial Deposit (Optional)**: If you already have cash set aside for this goal, enter an initial starting deposit.
5. **Icon & Category Color**: Select a representative icon (Emergency, Laptop, Travel, Home, Gift, Savings) and color code for visual identification.

---

### Step 3: Select Source Account (Optional Auto-Allocate)
- Choose whether initial funds should be deducted from an existing account (e.g., Bank Account or Cash).

---

### Step 4: Save & Activate
- Tap **Create Goal**. Your new goal card will immediately appear on the My Goals dashboard.

---

## Pro Tips for Goal Setting
- **Start Small**: Build momentum with achievable short-term goals (e.g., $100 rainy-day fund) before tackling multi-year goals.
- **Set Realistic Target Dates**: Piggy Ledger automatically shows required monthly contributions based on your target date. Make sure the monthly target aligns with your average monthly cash flow savings!
