# Net Worth & Summary Cards - User Guide

## What is Net Worth in Piggy Ledger?
Your **Net Worth** is the truest representation of your financial health. In **Piggy Ledger**, it is calculated by automatically adding up the positive balances of all your active accounts (banks, e-wallets, cash, investments) and subtracting any outstanding loans or debts you owe to others.

---

## Component Details & Breakdown

### 1. Total Net Worth Summary Card
- **What it shows**: Your overall financial standing (Assets minus Liabilities).
- **Why it matters**: Looking at a single account balance can give a false impression of wealth if you also carry debts. Net worth gives you an honest picture of your financial foundation.
- **How to interact**:
  - Tap on the Net Worth card to see a detailed modal listing total assets vs. total liabilities.
  - Use the **Privacy Toggle (Eye Icon)** to hide exact numbers with asterisks (`••••••`) when in public view.

---

### 2. Monthly Cash Flow (Income vs. Expense)
- **Income Display**: Green indicator displaying all earnings logged in the current calendar month.
- **Expense Display**: Red/Orange indicator showing all expenditures logged in the current calendar month.
- **Cash Flow Delta**: The difference between monthly income and expenses. If positive, you are living within your means and accumulating savings!

---

## How to Get the Most Out of Net Worth Tracking

### 1. Keep Account Balances Updated
Ensure every bank account, mobile wallet (e.g., Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, CIB, NBE), and physical cash pouch is represented under **Accounts**. Accurate balances result in an accurate Net Worth calculation.

### 2. Log Loans and Lends Accurately
When you borrow money or lend money to someone, record it in **Payoffs & Loans**. Piggy Ledger will factor borrowed funds into liabilities, keeping your net worth realistic.

### 3. Review Monthly Cash Flow Progress
Compare your cash flow card at the middle of the month vs. the end of the month to spot spending spikes early before they impact your savings goals.
