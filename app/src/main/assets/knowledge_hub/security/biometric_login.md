# Biometric Login & PIN Lock - User Guide

## Overview
Biometric unlock provides instant, secure access to Piggy Ledger using your phone's built-in biometric hardware (Fingerprint or Face Recognition) or a PIN passcode.

---

## How to Enable Biometric Lock

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open Piggy Ledger and tap the **Settings** icon on the Home screen.
2. Select **Security & Privacy**.
3. Toggle on **Enable App Lock**.
4. Choose your preferred authentication method:
   - **Biometrics (Fingerprint / Face ID)**: Uses your system biometrics.
   - **Custom App PIN**: Set a unique 4-digit numeric code.
5. Set **Auto-Lock Timeout**:
   - **Immediately**: Locks instantly when switching apps or locking phone screen.
   - **After 1 Minute**: Gives a 60-second window before requiring re-authentication.
   - **After 5 Minutes**: For active tracking sessions.

---

## What Happens When Locked?
- When you launch Piggy Ledger or return to it from another application, a clean lock screen prompts for fingerprint scan or PIN entry.
- If biometric scan fails multiple times, the app falls back to system PIN entry.

---

## What If You Forget Your PIN?
Because your data is private and offline-first, Piggy Ledger uses Android system biometric prompt fallbacks. You can authenticate using your primary Android device lock password or device biometric scan to regain access smoothly!
