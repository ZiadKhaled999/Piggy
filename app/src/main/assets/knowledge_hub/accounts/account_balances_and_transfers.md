# Account Balances & Transfers - User Guide

## Overview
Moving money between your own accounts—such as withdrawing cash from an ATM or funding a mobile wallet—is a **Transfer**, not an expense. Piggy Ledger handles inter-account transfers seamlessly without distorting your spending analytics or net worth.

---

## Performing an Inter-Account Transfer

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Tap **+ Add Transaction** and select **Transfer** (or tap **Transfer** directly from the Accounts screen).
2. Enter the **Transfer Amount**.
3. **From Account**: Select the originating account (e.g., `CIB Bank`).
4. **To Account**: Select the destination account (e.g., `Vodafone Cash` or `Personal Cash`).
5. **Transfer Fee (Optional)**: If the transaction incurs a service fee (e.g., ATM withdrawal fee or wallet transfer fee), enter the fee amount. Piggy Ledger will log the fee separately as an expense while keeping the main transfer neutral.
6. Add an optional note (e.g., "ATM Cash withdrawal for weekend").
7. Tap **Confirm Transfer**.

---

## How Balances Update
- **Source Account Balance**: Decreases by `Transfer Amount + Fee`.
- **Destination Account Balance**: Increases by `Transfer Amount`.
- **Net Worth**: Remains completely unchanged (except for any fee logged).
- **Analytics**: Excluded from expense/income totals, ensuring your monthly budget remains accurate!

---

## Reconciling Balances (Balance Audit)
If your real-life bank account balance ever differs from Piggy Ledger (due to a forgotten purchase or unrecorded coffee):
1. Open the specific **Account Details** page.
2. Tap **Adjust Balance** / **Quick Balance Sync**.
3. Enter your current real-world balance.
4. Piggy Ledger will log a small balancing entry so your records match reality instantly.
