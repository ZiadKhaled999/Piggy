# Piggy Ledger Pro - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
**Piggy Ledger Pro** is the premium upgrade tier designed for users who want complete financial power, advanced automation, unlimited data backups, receipt scanning, and custom analytics. While Piggy Ledger offers a solid free core, upgrading to **Pro** unlocks the full potential of your financial journey.

---

## What is Included in Piggy Ledger Pro?

1. **Unlimited Accounts & Goals**: No caps on how many bank accounts, cash wallets, or savings goals you can track simultaneously.
2. **Automated Receipt Attachment**: Scan and store photos of paper receipts directly on your transactions.
3. **Advanced Analytics & Exporting**: Export detailed financial reports in CSV or PDF formats for accounting or tax season.
4. **Streak Protection Badges**: Unlimited monthly streak freeze shields to protect your logging streak.
5. **Encrypted Cloud Backup & Sync**: Seamless cloud synchronization across multiple personal devices.
6. **Priority Customer Support & Zero Ads**: Clean, uninterrupted user experience with direct support access.
