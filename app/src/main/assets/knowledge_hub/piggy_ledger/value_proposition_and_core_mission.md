# Value Proposition & Core Mission - User Guide

## Our Core Mission
Managing money should not require a degree in accounting or hours spent in spreadsheets. **Piggy Ledger** was created to democratize financial clarity by replacing confusing banking statements with a clean, friendly, and empowering visual ledger.

---

## What Problems Does Piggy Ledger Solve?

### 1. The "Where Did My Money Go?" Problem
- **The Issue**: Money scattered across multiple bank cards, cash, and digital wallets (Vodafone Cash, InstaPay) makes it easy to lose track of spending.
- **The Piggy Solution**: A single Net Worth indicator and unified recent activity feed that consolidates every single account balance in real time.

---

### 2. Manual Data Entry Fatigue
- **The Issue**: People start budgeting apps with enthusiasm but give up after a week because typing every receipt manually is tiring.
- **The Piggy Solution**: Automatic SMS parsing stages bank and wallet transactions into a **Pending Inbox**. Approving a transaction takes one tap.

---

### 3. Forgotten Debts & Awkward Loans
- **The Issue**: Informal lending between friends or family leads to forgotten balances or awkward conversations.
- **The Piggy Solution**: Dedicated **Payoffs & Loans** ledger tracking exact principals, partial repayments, and remaining balances transparently.

---

### 4. Unfocused Savings
- **The Issue**: Saving money without a clear target often results in dipping into savings for everyday purchases.
- **The Piggy Solution**: Goal piggy banks with clear progress bars and target completion dates keep funds earmarked for specific intentions.
