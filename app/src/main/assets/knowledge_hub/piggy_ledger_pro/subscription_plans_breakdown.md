# Subscription Plans Breakdown - User Guide

## Overview
Piggy Ledger Pro offers three flexible tiers designed to suit your budget and personal preference.

---

## 1. Monthly Subscription Plan
- **Billing Cycle**: Billed once every month.
- **Ideal For**: Users who want to test out Pro features short-term or prefer low initial commitment.
- **Cancellation**: Cancel anytime with one tap through Google Play Subscriptions.

---

## 2. Annual Subscription Plan (Best Value / Recommended)
- **Billing Cycle**: Billed once per year (equivalent to **17% savings** compared to monthly pricing).
- **Free Trial**: Includes a 7-day risk-free trial period.
- **Ideal For**: Serious financial trackers who want long-term habit building at the most cost-effective annual rate.

---

## 3. Lifetime Purchase Plan
- **Billing Cycle**: Single one-time payment for permanent access. Never pay a subscription fee again!
- **Ideal For**: Users who dislike recurring monthly subscriptions and want permanent access to all present and future Pro updates.
