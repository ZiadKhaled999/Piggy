# Payoffs & Loans - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
The **Payoffs & Loans Screen** in **Piggy Ledger** provides total transparency over money you owe (**Liabilities / Debts**) and money owed to you by others (**Assets / Lends**). Managing personal debts and informal loans among friends, family, or institutions can easily get chaotic; Piggy Ledger keeps every single transaction, partial payment, and remaining balance crystal clear.

---

## Debt vs. Lend Classification

### 1. I Owe Someone (Borrowed / Debt)
- **Definition**: Money you borrowed from a person (friend, colleague) or financial institution that you must pay back.
- **Impact on Net Worth**: Counted as a **Liability** (reduces Net Worth).

---

### 2. Someone Owes Me (Lent / Asset)
- **Definition**: Money you lent out to a friend or relative that they are scheduled to repay you.
- **Impact on Net Worth**: Counted as an **Receivable Asset** (part of Net Worth).

---

## Core Features of Payoffs & Loans

1. **Detailed Person Ledger**: Track loans by counterparty name (e.g., `Loan to Ahmed`, `Debt to Cousin`).
2. **Partial Payment Tracking**: Log installment payments over time (e.g., someone paying back $300 out of a $500 loan).
3. **Automatic Balance Calculation**: Live recalculation of total principal, total paid, and remaining balance.
4. **DueDate & Reminder Alerts**: Set payoff deadlines with automatic notification reminders so obligations are never forgotten.
5. **Settlement & Archiving**: One-tap full settlement once a loan is paid off in full.

---

## Why Track Loans in Piggy Ledger?
- Eliminates awkward disputes over "how much is left to pay".
- Accurately adjusts your total net worth so you don't overcount lent money as available spending cash.
