# Reviewing & Approving Pending Transactions - User Guide

## Overview
The **Pending Transactions Inbox** displays all automatically parsed messages waiting for your confirmation.

---

## How to Process Pending Items

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Tap the **Pending Transactions** card on the Home dashboard or navigate via **Settings** > **Pending Inbox**.
2. A list of staged card entries will appear.

---

### Actions Available per Item:

#### 1. Quick Approve (Green Checkmark)
- If the parsed amount, account, and category look correct, tap **Approve**.
- The transaction moves into your main ledger, account balances update instantly, and your continuous logging streak increases!

#### 2. Edit & Approve (Pencil Icon)
- If you want to change the auto-assigned category (e.g., from *General* to *Dining Out*) or refine the merchant note, tap **Edit**.
- Make your adjustments and tap **Save & Approve**.

#### 3. Dismiss / Delete (Red Trash Icon)
- If an SMS notification was a promotional message, bank alert, or duplicate entry, tap **Dismiss**.
- The item is removed from the pending inbox without altering your accounts or net worth.

---

## Batch Operations (Approve All)
If you have multiple verified pending transactions from a shopping trip or banking day, tap **Approve All** at the top right to confirm the entire list in one tap!
