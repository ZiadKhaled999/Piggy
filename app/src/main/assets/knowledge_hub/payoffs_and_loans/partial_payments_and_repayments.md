# Partial Payments & Repayments - User Guide

## Handling Partial Repayments
Loans are rarely paid back in a single lump sum. Piggy Ledger makes logging partial repayments simple and error-free.

---

## Example Scenario:
> **Scenario**: You lent **$500** to your friend Omar. Today, Omar pays you back **$300** via InstaPay, leaving **$200** outstanding.

---

## How to Log a Partial Repayment

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to **Payoffs & Loans**.
2. Tap on the active loan card (e.g., `Loan to Omar - Original $500`).
3. Tap **Log Payment** / **Add Repayment**.
4. Key in the repayment amount: `300`.
5. Select the **Receiving Account**: `InstaPay` (where Omar sent the money).
6. Set Payment Date & optional note (e.g., "First installment via InstaPay").
7. Tap **Confirm Payment**.

---

## What Updates Automatically:
1. **Loan Remaining Balance**: Instantly drops from **$500** to **$200**.
2. **Receiving Account Balance**: `InstaPay` balance increases by **+$300**.
3. **Loan Payment History**: A clear chronological log records:
   - Initial Principal: $500
   - Repayment #1: +$300 (Date: Today)
   - Remaining: $200

---

## Settling a Loan in Full
When the final payment of $200 is logged, Piggy Ledger marks the loan status as **Fully Settled 🎉** and moves it to the Settled Loans archive.
