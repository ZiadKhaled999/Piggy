# Language & Localization - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
**Piggy Ledger** is built with full native multi-language support, featuring native **English** and **Arabic (العربية)** localizations. Personal financial management is most comfortable in your primary native language; Piggy Ledger supports right-to-left (RTL) layouts seamlessly for Arabic users.

---

## Supported Languages

### 1. English (US)
- Complete English interface, numerical notation, date formatting, and category naming.

---

### 2. Arabic - Egypt / General (العربية)
- Full **Right-to-Left (RTL)** layout mirroring.
- Localized financial terminology, Egyptian Bank presets, and cultural context.
- Custom Arabic numbers (`١٢٣`) or standard Western numerals (`123`) option depending on your preference.

---

## Core Advantages of Piggy Ledger Localization
- **Instant Switching**: Switch languages on the fly without restarting the app or losing any transaction records.
- **RTL Adaptive Layout**: UI buttons, progress bars, and navigation drawers reverse orientation naturally to follow native Arabic reading patterns.
