# Streaks & Gamification - User Guide

## What is the Streak System?
Building good money habits requires consistency. The **Streak System** in Piggy Ledger turns daily financial logging into an engaging, gamified experience. Every consecutive day you log a transaction or engage with the app, your daily streak increases!

---

## Streak Features & UI Components

### 1. Active Streak Counter
- **Visual Display**: Flame icon with your current streak day count (e.g., `7 Days Streak 🔥`).
- **How it builds**: Logs increase by +1 every day you log at least one income, expense, transfer, or goal deposit.

---

### 2. Streak Calendar & History Board
- **Visual Display**: A 7-day rolling calendar row showing active days (green checkmarks), missed days (red cross), or frozen days (ice shield).
- **How to interact**: Tap the streak counter on the Home screen to open the **Streak & Achievements Screen**.

---

### 3. Streak Freeze Badges
- **What is a Streak Freeze?**: A protective shield that prevents your streak count from resetting to zero if you accidentally forget to log a transaction for a single day.
- **How to obtain**: Unlocked automatically as milestone rewards or provided with Piggy Ledger Pro.
- **How it works**: Automatically consumes a freeze badge if a day passes without activity, keeping your streak intact!

---

### 4. Piggy Moods & Milestones
- **Piggy Mascot Reactions**: Piggy's expression changes based on your habit consistency and goal progress!
  - **Happy Piggy**: High streak and active savings.
  - **Panic / Worried Piggy**: High monthly expenses exceeding budget limits or inactive tracking.
  - **Pool Piggy**: Unlocked major financial goals!

---

## Pro Tips for Maintaining Your Streak
1. **Enable Notification Reminders**: Set a daily evening reminder in **Settings** so you never forget to log your daily expenses.
2. **Use SMS Auto-Parsing**: When SMS auto-parsing detects transactions, simply tap "Approve" from pending transactions to maintain your streak effortlessly.
