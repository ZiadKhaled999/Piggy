# Pro Features & Value Proposition - User Guide

## Why Upgrade to Piggy Ledger Pro?

### 1. Save Hours of Manual Accounting
With automatic SMS parsing, unlimited bank account identifiers, and automated cloud sync, Piggy Ledger Pro cuts weekly budgeting time down from hours to minutes.

---

### 2. Protect Your Wealth with Data Exports
Exporting your financial data to CSV or Excel allows you to perform deep tax analysis, share statements with accountants, or keep independent records outside your smartphone.

---

### 3. Never Lose Financial Data
Phone upgrades, misplaced devices, or hardware failure can erase unbacked data. Pro Cloud Sync automatically mirrors your encrypted database so your financial history is always recoverable.

---

### 4. Psychological Motivation
Investing in a dedicated financial tool reinforces your commitment to money discipline. Users who upgrade to Pro report saving an average of **15% to 25% more money** in their first 3 months by staying active with unlimited savings goals!

---

## How to Upgrade
1. Tap the **Piggy Ledger Pro** banner on the Settings or Home screen.
2. Select your preferred tier (**Monthly**, **Annual**, or **Lifetime**).
3. Confirm purchase via Google Play store checkout. Your Pro features unlock instantly!
