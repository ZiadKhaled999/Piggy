# Tracking Goal Progress & Milestones - User Guide

## Overview
Piggy Ledger makes tracking savings progress dynamic and satisfying. This guide explains how goal progress is calculated, how milestones work, and how to analyze your savings speed.

---

## Understanding Goal Metrics

### 1. Progress Bar & Completion Percentage
- **Percentage Formula**: `(Current Saved Balance / Target Goal Amount) * 100`.
- **Visual Progress Bar**: Changes color dynamically as you get closer to your target:
  - **0% - 30%**: Initial Phase (Blue)
  - **31% - 70%**: Steady Progress (Orange/Gold)
  - **71% - 99%**: Final Stretch (Teal)
  - **100%**: Completed Goal (Gold/Green Sparkle 🎉)

---

### 2. Time vs. Progress Indicator
- Compares your **time elapsed** against your **savings progress**.
- If you have spent 50% of the allocated timeframe but saved 60% of the target, you are **Ahead of Schedule**!
- If you have spent 70% of the timeframe but saved only 30%, Piggy Ledger displays a helpful indicator suggesting adjusted daily deposits.

---

## Goal Milestones & Achievements
Piggy Ledger rewards progress at major savings checkpoints:
- **Quarter Mark (25%)**: Unlocks the "First Step" badge.
- **Halfway Point (50%)**: Unlocks the "Halfway Hero" mascot graphic.
- **Final Stretch (75%)**: Unlocks the "In Sight" motivation banner.
- **Goal Reached (100%)**: Unlocks full victory animation, badge, and streak bonus!

---

## Archiving & Reviewing Past Goals
Once a goal is achieved, you can move it to **Archived Goals**. You can always review past completed goals to celebrate your history of financial discipline and savings achievements!
