# Income & Expense Breakdown - User Guide

## Overview
Detailed breakdown reports allow you to inspect income streams and expense categories independently for precise budgeting.

---

## Expense Category Breakdown

### How to Read the Expense Pie Chart:
1. **Dominant Slices**: The largest slices represent your highest expense categories (e.g., Housing, Groceries, Dining Out).
2. **Category Percentage**: Displays what percentage of your total monthly outflow went to that category (e.g., `Dining Out: 32% of total expenses`).
3. **Drill-Down Inspection**: Tap any category slice or list item to view all individual transaction receipts recorded under that category during the selected period.

---

## Income Source Breakdown
Toggle the chart view from **Expense** to **Income** to analyze your cash inflows:
- **Salary / Wage**: Primary recurring employment income.
- **Freelance / Side Business**: Project earnings and business revenues.
- **Investments & Refunds**: Dividends, interest, or store refunds.
- **Transfers & Gifts**: Monetary gifts or incoming peer transfers.

---

## Pro Tip for Category Optimization
If your **Uncategorized** or **Other** category is larger than 10%, review past transactions and assign specific tags. Precise categorization leads to clearer financial insights!
