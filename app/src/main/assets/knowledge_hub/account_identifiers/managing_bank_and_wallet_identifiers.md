# Managing Bank & Wallet Identifiers - User Guide

## Overview
You can easily add, edit, or delete SMS identifiers attached to any account in Piggy Ledger.

---

## How to Add a New Identifier to an Account

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open Piggy Ledger and go to **Settings** > **Account Identifiers** (or open **Accounts** > Edit Account).
2. Select the **Account** you wish to configure (e.g., `CIB Checking Account`).
3. Scroll down to the **SMS Identifiers** section.
4. Tap **+ Add New Identifier**.
5. Type the exact sender title that appears on your phone's SMS inbox (e.g., `CIB-ALERT` or `1234`).
6. Tap **Save**.

---

## How to Delete or Remove an Identifier
1. Under **Account Identifiers**, find the tag listed under your account.
2. Tap the **X / Remove** icon next to the identifier tag.
3. Confirm deletion.

---

## Pro Tip for Multi-Card Users
If you have two credit cards under the same bank (e.g., CIB Gold Card ending in `4321` and CIB Platinum Card ending in `8765`), you can add specific card digits (`4321`) as separate identifiers to route transactions to different account cards in Piggy Ledger!
