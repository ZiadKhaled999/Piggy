# Adding a Loan or Debt - Step-by-Step Guide

## Overview
Whether you lent $500 to a friend or took out a personal loan, logging it correctly ensures your financial records stay accurate.

---

## How to Add a New Loan Record

### Step 1: Open Loans Screen
- Navigate to the **Payoffs & Loans** section from the navigation menu.
- Tap the **+ Add Loan / Debt** button.

---

### Step 2: Choose Loan Direction
Select one of the two options:
- **I Lent Money** (Someone owes me): Money left your account as a loan.
- **I Borrowed Money** (I owe someone): Money entered your account as a debt.

---

### Step 3: Enter Counterparty & Amount Details
1. **Person / Institution Name**: Name of the borrower or lender (e.g., `Mohamed Ali`, `Bank Personal Loan`).
2. **Principal Amount**: The original total amount borrowed or lent (e.g., `$500` or `EGP 10,000`).
3. **Associated Account**: Select which account was used to disburse or receive the money (e.g., `InstaPay` or `Cash`).
4. **Loan Date**: Date the loan was agreed upon or transferred.
5. **Due Date (Optional)**: Agreed deadline for full repayment.
6. **Notes & Tags**: Add relevant context (e.g., "Dinner bill split reimbursement" or "Car repair loan").

---

### Step 4: Save Loan
- Tap **Save Loan**. The loan entry is created, updating your account balance and loan summary dashboard immediately.
