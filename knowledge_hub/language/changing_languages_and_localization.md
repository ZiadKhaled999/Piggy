# Changing Languages & Localization - Step-by-Step Guide

## Overview
You can customize your language preference during onboarding or anytime within App Settings.

---

## How to Change Application Language

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Launch **Piggy Ledger** and open **Settings** from the Home screen.
2. Select **Language Settings** (or **اللغة**).
3. Choose your desired language:
   - **English**
   - **العربية (Arabic)**
   - **Follow System Default** (Automatically matches your Android system device language).
4. Tap **Apply Language**.

---

## What Changes When You Switch to Arabic?
- **RTL Interface**: Navigation bars, account cards, and back arrows align to the right side of the screen.
- **Localized Categories**: Categories update to clear Arabic equivalents (e.g., *طعام ومشروبات*, *فواتير*, *مواصلات*, *راتب*).
- **Date & Calendar Formatting**: Dates adapt to local Arabic date conventions.

---

## Pro Tip for Dual Language Users
If you receive bank SMS notifications in English but prefer browsing Piggy Ledger in Arabic, SMS auto-parsing works across both languages seamlessly!
