# Financial Insights & Trends - User Guide

## Overview
Piggy Ledger goes beyond simple totals by analyzing monthly trends and generating smart financial insights tailored to your habit history.

---

## Key Trend Metrics

### 1. Month-over-Month (MoM) Spending Trend
- Compares your current month’s total spending against the previous month at the exact same day mark.
- **Example Alert**: `You have spent 15% less on Dining Out compared to this time last month! 👏`

---

### 2. Average Daily Expenditure
- Calculates how much money you spend per day on average during the current period.
- Formula: `Total Monthly Expense / Days Elapsed in Month`.
- Helps set a realistic daily allowance for discretionary spending.

---

### 3. Savings Rate Percentage
- Indicates what percentage of your total income was successfully saved rather than spent.
- Formula: `((Total Income - Total Expense) / Total Income) * 100`.
- Benchmark Goal: Aim for a savings rate of **20% or higher** for financial health!

---

## Smart Financial Tips & Mascot Callouts
Based on your analytics, Piggy Ledger's mascot will display contextual guidance on your dashboard:
- **Low Expense Month**: "Great job! You are on track to exceed your savings target this month!"
- **High Budget Spike**: "Heads up! Expense in 'Shopping' is higher than usual this week."
