# Pending Transactions - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
The **Pending Transactions Screen** is Piggy Ledger's automated expense assistant. Modern banks and digital wallet services send SMS text messages whenever a purchase, transfer, or salary payment occurs. Piggy Ledger reads these messages locally and automatically stages them as **Pending Transactions** for one-tap approval.

---

## What is a Pending Transaction?
A **Pending Transaction** is a transaction detected from an SMS notification that has not yet been added to your official database ledger. It acts as an inbox for incoming financial records.

---

## Core Advantages of Pending Transactions

1. **Zero Manual Data Entry**: Amounts, dates, merchant descriptions, and accounts are pre-filled automatically.
2. **Complete User Control**: Nothing enters your main ledger without your explicit review and confirmation.
3. **One-Tap Approval**: Approve incoming transactions in bulk or modify categories before accepting.
4. **Ignore / Dismiss Option**: Reject non-financial SMS messages, promo codes, or personal messages with a single tap.

---

## Supported Institutions & SMS Partners
Piggy Ledger recognizes SMS transaction patterns from major financial providers, including:
- **Bank Accounts**: CIB, National Bank of Egypt (NBE), Banque Misr, AlexBank, QNB, HSBC, ADIB, AAIB, etc.
- **Mobile Wallets**: Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash, WE Pay.
- **Payment Apps**: InstaPay.
