# Analytics Screen - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
The **Analytics Screen** in **Piggy Ledger** turns raw transaction numbers into visual insights. Understanding *where* your money goes is the single most effective step toward financial freedom. Analytics breaks down your spending patterns, income sources, category allocations, and monthly trends using intuitive charts and visual cards.

---

## Core Visualizations on the Analytics Screen

### 1. Interactive Pie Chart (Category Breakdown)
- Visual donut chart displaying percentage allocation across expense categories (Food, Bills, Rent, Shopping, Entertainment, Utilities, etc.).
- Tap any segment to isolate spending in that specific category.

---

### 2. Time-Period Selector
- Filter analytics by **This Week**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **Custom Date Range**, or **Year-to-Date**.

---

### 3. Income vs. Expense Comparison Bar Chart
- Side-by-side bar graph showing monthly earnings versus monthly spending over time, highlighting monthly savings capacity.

---

### 4. Category Detailed Breakdown Table
- Ranked list of top spending categories with total monetary amounts, transaction counts, and percentage of overall budget.

---

## Why Use Analytics Regularly?
- **Spot Money Leaks**: Identify small recurring expenses (like daily coffee runs or unused subscriptions) that add up to big totals over time.
- **Budget Adjustments**: Make informed decisions on where to cut back based on historical data rather than guesswork.
