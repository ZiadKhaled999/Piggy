# Logging Expenses & Incomes per Account - User Guide

## Overview
Logging income and expenses against specific accounts keeps your digital wallet and bank statement balances perfectly synced.

---

## How to Log an Expense to a Specific Account

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Tap the **+ Add Transaction** button anywhere in the app.
2. Ensure **Expense** is selected at the top.
3. Key in the monetary amount (e.g., `$25.00` or `EGP 450`).
4. **Select Account**: Choose the exact account used for payment (e.g., `Vodafone Cash`).
5. **Select Category**: Choose a fitting category (e.g., Groceries, Restaurant, Bills, Transportation, Healthcare, Shopping).
6. **Add Merchant / Note (Optional)**: Type vendor details (e.g., `Supermarket`, `Uber ride`).
7. **Attach Photo / Receipt (Pro Feature)**: Capture or attach a receipt image for bookkeeping.
8. Tap **Save Expense**.

---

## How to Log Income to an Account

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Tap **+ Add Transaction** and toggle to **Income**.
2. Key in the earnings amount (e.g., `$3,000` or `EGP 25,000`).
3. **Select Account**: Choose where the money was deposited (e.g., `CIB Checking`).
4. **Select Category**: Choose Income source (Salary, Freelance, Investment, Gift, Refund).
5. **Set Date**: Select payment date.
6. Tap **Save Income**.

---

## Automatic SMS Account Allocation
If you have SMS parser permissions enabled, incoming bank transaction SMS messages (e.g., from `CIB` or `Vodafone Cash`) will automatically pre-select the matching account when creating pending transactions!
