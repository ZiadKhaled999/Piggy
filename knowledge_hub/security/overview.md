# Security Screen - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
Your financial data is deeply personal. The **Security Settings** in **Piggy Ledger** give you total control over privacy, access lockouts, device-level protection, and sensitive data visibility. Piggy Ledger operates with a strong commitment to privacy: your financial data remains stored safely on your device under your protection.

---

## Core Security Features

### 1. Biometric & Passcode Lock
- Require **Fingerprint Authentication**, **Face Unlock**, or a secure 4-digit PIN every time the app opens.

---

### 2. Screen Protection (Prevent Screenshots)
- Block screenshot capture and hide app preview snapshots in Android's recent apps switcher screen.

---

### 3. Mask Sensitive Balances (Privacy Mode)
- Automatically hide exact monetary values with asterisks (`••••••`) across all screens until explicitly tapped.

---

### 4. Local Data Security & Backups
- Export encrypted local database backups to your device or private personal cloud storage without exposing raw credentials.

---

## Why Configure Security Features?
- Prevents friends or strangers from viewing your balance when handing over your phone.
- Shields financial accounts in public settings (cafes, transit).
- Ensures unauthorized users cannot open the app if your device is unlocked.
