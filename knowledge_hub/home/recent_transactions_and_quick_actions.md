# Recent Transactions & Quick Actions - User Guide

## Overview
The **Quick Action Bar** and **Recent Activity Feed** on the Home screen provide the fastest way to log, manage, and review everyday financial movements without extra clicks.

---

## Quick Actions

### 1. Add Transaction Button (+ Icon)
- **Primary Use**: Instant entry for expenses or incomes.
- **Fields & Controls**:
  - **Amount Entry**: Large numeric keypad for quick entry.
  - **Transaction Type**: Toggle between Expense, Income, or Transfer.
  - **Account Selector**: Pick which bank or wallet was used.
  - **Category Chooser**: Visual grid of categories (Food, Utilities, Transportation, Salary, Shopping, etc.).
  - **Date & Time Picker**: Defaults to right now, but backdating is supported.
  - **Note & Tags**: Add brief descriptions or tags for searchability later.

---

### 2. Account Transfer Action
- **Primary Use**: Move money from one account to another (e.g., ATM Cash Withdrawal from Bank Account, or transfer to mobile wallet like Vodafone Cash).
- **Benefit**: Keeps individual account balances 100% accurate without affecting your net worth or logging an unnecessary "expense".

---

## Recent Activity Feed

### 1. Item Cards Breakdown
Each item card displays:
- **Category Icon & Color**: Visually identifies the expense/income category at a glance.
- **Title / Merchant Name**: Description or merchant tag.
- **Account Label**: Indicates which bank or wallet was affected (e.g., `CIB Bank`, `Cash`).
- **Date Stamp**: Relative time (Today, Yesterday, or specific date).
- **Signed Amount**:
  - **Green (+)**: Income received.
  - **Red (-)**: Expense spent.
  - **Blue/Gray (⇄)**: Internal transfer between accounts.

---

### 2. Interactions & Editing
- **Tap to View/Edit**: Tap any transaction row to bring up full details. You can modify the amount, change category, adjust dates, or delete the transaction.
- **Swipe Actions**: Fast swipe gestures to delete or edit records directly from the list.
- **Search & Filter**: Tap "See All" to open the advanced ledger with date ranges, category filters, and keyword search.
