# Getting Started & Best Practices - User Guide

## Quick Start Blueprint for New Users

Follow these 5 steps to get the maximum value out of Piggy Ledger from Day 1:

---

### Step 1: Set Up Your Accounts (5 Minutes)
- Head to **Accounts** and create an entry for every location where you hold money:
  - Physical Cash wallet
  - Bank Accounts (e.g., CIB, NBE)
  - Mobile Wallets (e.g., Vodafone Cash)
  - InstaPay / Digital accounts
- Input your real-world starting balance for each account.

---

### Step 2: Enable SMS Automation & Identifiers
- Go to **Settings** > **SMS Automation** and enable bank SMS read permissions.
- Verify your bank sender titles under **Account Identifiers** so pending transactions route to the correct account automatically.

---

### Step 3: Create Your First Savings Goal
- Go to **Goals** and tap **+ Create Goal**.
- Set up an achievable short-term goal (e.g., `$100 Emergency Cushion` or `Vacation Fund`).

---

### Step 4: Secure Your App
- Go to **Settings** > **Security & Privacy** and turn on **Biometric Lock** and **Screen Protection**.

---

### Step 5: Build Your Daily Logging Habit
- Open the app once every evening or approve pending SMS items to maintain your **Daily Streak**!

---

## Pro User Best Practices

1. **Use Transfers for Internal Money Movement**: Moving money from bank to cash is a *Transfer*, not an *Expense*.
2. **Review Analytics Weekly**: Check the **Analytics** pie chart every Sunday to stay aware of spending trends.
3. **Log Loan Repayments Immediately**: Whenever someone pays back part of a debt, log it right away in **Payoffs & Loans** to keep your net worth accurate.
4. **Backup Regularly**: If on the free plan, export local backups under **Settings** once a month. Pro users enjoy automated cloud backups!
