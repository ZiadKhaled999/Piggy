# Goals Screen - Knowledge Hub Overview

## Introduction
The **Goals Screen** ("My Goals") in **Piggy Ledger** empowers you to transform long-term dreams into achievable, bite-sized targets. Whether saving for an emergency fund, a new smartphone, a vacation, a down payment, or a major purchase, Piggy Ledger helps you stay motivated, track saved funds, and hit your milestones step-by-step.

---

## What are Savings Goals?
Unlike regular account balances, a **Goal** acts as a virtual piggy bank. It allows you to set aside money for specific purposes without spending it in daily transactions.

---

## Core Features of the Goals Screen

### 1. Goal Cards Grid
- Displays every target you are currently saving for.
- Visual elements include target progress bars, percentage completed, target date countdowns, and custom icons.

---

### 2. Goal Creation Wizard
- Easily set up custom targets with flexible target amounts, target completion dates, priority levels, and custom icons/colors.

---

### 3. Smart Deposit & Withdrawal Controls
- Transfer funds directly from your accounts into a goal piggy bank.
- Withdraw funds back to an account if unexpected expenses arise or when the goal is completed.

---

### 4. Milestone Celebrations & Analytics
- Visual achievements, mascot celebrations, and target completion alerts when you reach 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your savings goal!

---

## Why Use Savings Goals?
- **Intentional Saving**: Earmarks funds for specific needs so you don't accidentally spend money meant for rent, emergency savings, or vacations.
- **Visual Encouragement**: Seeing progress bars fill up gives a psychological boost and keeps financial discipline strong.
